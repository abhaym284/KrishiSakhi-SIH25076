Crop Guide & Advisory

Detailed agricultural guidance, stage requirements, pest identification and nutrient management.

Rice (धान / ડાંગર)

Staple cereal crop. Essential guide for moisture management, stem borer control, and blast disease prevention.

🌾

Wheat (गेहूं / ઘઉં)

Major rabi cereal crop. Guidance on rust diseases, leaf blight, and timely irrigation stages.

🌿

Cotton (कपास / કપાસ)

Major commercial fiber crop. Management of pink bollworm, whitefly, and wilt diseases.

🥜

Groundnut (मूंगफली / મગફળી)

Important oilseed crop. Focus on tikka leaf spot, stem rot, and pegging moisture needs.

🥬

Vegetables (सब्जियां / શાકભાજી)

High value horticulture. Pest management for tomato, brinjal, chilli, and cucurbits.
