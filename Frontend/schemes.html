Farmer Government Schemes

Explore agricultural subsidies, insurance schemes, and government financial support.

Note: Scheme information provided is for educational & guidance purposes.

Central Scheme

PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

Direct income support to all landholding farmer families across the country to assist with agricultural input purchases.

Benefits: ₹6,000 per year in 3 equal installments directly into bank accounts.
Central Insurance

PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana)

Comprehensive crop insurance coverage against un-preventable natural risks from pre-sowing to post-harvest.

Benefits: Minimal premium rates (1.5% - 2%) for farmers, full sum insured coverage against crop loss.
State Scheme

Micro Irrigation Scheme (PMKSY - Per Drop More Crop)

Financial assistance and subsidies for installing Drip and Sprinkler irrigation systems to improve water use efficiency.

Benefits: 70% - 85% subsidy on drip and sprinkler micro-irrigation installation.
Soil Health

Soil Health Card Scheme

Provides farmers with customized soil nutrient status report and crop-wise fertilizer recommendations.

Benefits: Free soil testing and tailored fertilizer dosage advice to lower input costs.
